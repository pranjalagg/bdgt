    How patterns work

    Each pair of everyday envelopes is compared month by month: do the months where one is high tend to be the months where the other is low (or high too)? Rent, subscriptions, savings and one-off envelopes are left out.

    It compares which months were high or low, not by how much, so one unusual month can't create a pattern on its own. With only a few months of history, a lean is shown as "watching" and not as a finding.

    It describes what happened. It can't tell you why.

    bdgt, rethought for the phone

    A clickable concept with your sample October. Nothing in the real app has changed.

    1. Press and slide on the chart on Home to see how much you had spent by any day.
    2. Tap the payments card, then swipe cards right to approve and left to dismiss.
    3. Tap + and type an amount on the keypad; try 12 + 8.5. Pick an envelope and watch Home's figure roll.
    4. On Activity, swipe a row left to delete or right to move it.
    5. On Budget, tap an envelope, then swipe from the left edge to go back. Tap Assign and take it to zero.
    6. Edit Home at the bottom of Home turns cards on and off.
    7. On Insights, tap a trend card to open its chart, then press and slide to read any month. Tap $500 or more to change what counts as a big purchase.
    8. The avatar opens Settings, where Appearance switches light and dark.
